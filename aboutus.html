The Visionary Behind the Brand

Joseph Aljon Dee

"Success is most beautiful when it serves a purpose beyond profit—it's about fulfilling dreams and building a legacy of care."

An experienced entrepreneur and the management force behind North Walk Pampanga’s Bach n Barrel, Joseph founded Jadeluxe in July 2023. By merging his business expertise with his sister’s medical mastery, he has created a sanctuary where professional management meets clinical excellence.

Where We Began

The Jadeluxe Origin

Jadeluxe was born from a son’s deep appreciation for his mother’s love for wellness. For years, Joseph watched his mother find joy in the transformative power of self-care and aesthetic treatments. He didn't just want to build a business; he wanted to bring his mother's dream to life.

To ensure this dream was built on a foundation of professional safety and medical expertise, Joseph collaborated with his sister, Dr. Jade, a dedicated cosmetic surgeon. Together, they combined the warmth of a family legacy with the precision of modern medicine.

Today, Jadeluxe stands as a premier destination in Region 3, offering a unique blend of salon, wellness, and advanced aesthetic services that make premium beauty accessible to all.
